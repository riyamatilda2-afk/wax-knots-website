Frequently Asked Questions

How long do the candles burn?

Our premium candles are designed to provide 30-50 hours of burn time depending on the size. For best results, trim the wick to 1/4 inch before each use.

Are your candles eco-friendly?

Yes! We use 100% natural soy wax and eco-friendly packaging materials. Our candles are sustainable and safe for your home.

What is your return policy?

We offer a 7-day return policy for unused candles in original packaging. If you receive a damaged product, please notify us within 48 hours with photos.

How fast is the delivery?

We aim to ship all orders within 24-48 hours. Delivery typically takes 3-5 business days depending on your location.
